# Contributor Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Short version

- Be respectful. Disagreements about code, design and product are fine; personal attacks are not.
- No harassment, discrimination, or sexualized comments. Period.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming malice.
- Maintainer decisions are final.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer (see `git log` for contact). Reports are confidential.
